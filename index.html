Active Hunt — discovery crawler

What this does: instead of you pasting URLs, the crawler actively goes and finds the material. It hunts two ways at once: (1) text — search queries built from your watchlist + high-severity codebook terms; and (2) image-first — reverse-image / perceptual-hash matching that finds re-posts of a known-bad image (a flag, a media-affiliate logo, a corner watermark) across many accounts, even cropped or re-saved. It scores each candidate and drops threats into the review queue for a human. Nothing is auto-reported or auto-actioned. Connectors use official platform APIs / approved access only — no private content, no credential stuffing. Your own model can be plugged in as the finder. Demo mode uses a synthetic connector (no live calls).
Image-first discovery — for image-borne threats (the dominant pattern: the same flag/logo/watermark re-posted over and over), the strongest signal isn't words, it's the picture. Add reference images in the Library tab; each becomes a perceptual-hash watch, and every sweep hunts the network for re-posts of it. Confirmed hits add their own images back to the watch set, so coverage compounds.

📎Important notes about search access▾
✅ The system searches these itself
Telegram — public channels, real messages
Bluesky — posts and handles
Mastodon / fediverse — public hashtag feeds
8kun — thread text only (images are never downloaded — those boards carry illegal material)
Odysee — video titles and channels
Open web — finds accounts, storefronts, mirrors and reporting
⛔ These cannot be searched — here's why
Facebook & Instagram — Meta blocks every crawler. Nobody can search these, at any price, without Meta Content Library access. Capture them yourself: paste the link in the box at the top.
X / Twitter — X charges for search (about $100–200 a month). Accounts still turn up; their individual posts don't.
Snapchat — no public search exists at all. Not blocked, not paid — it simply isn't offered.
Gab · Rumble — both refuse automated access.
If a search comes back empty, it usually means the platform is on the right-hand list — not that there is nothing out there. For anything on the right, capture it by hand: copy the link and paste it into the box at the top, or upload a screenshot.
